# Actor Visual Effects

The battle presenter owns a shared AVE entry point for status state and status grants. Brian's procedural barrier implementation lives in `BattleStagePresenter.ActorVisualEffects.cs`; any actor with shield receives the same barrier, activation, absorption and break visuals. Existing stance and readiness effects retain their behavior.

`SyncActorVisualEffects` consumes the displayed fighter snapshot. Stun is identified by its recipe ID or `control.stun` tag, and shows three orbiting stars with a slight bowed, wavering model pose. At that team's turn entry, stunned actors flinch simultaneously. Discarding a blocked stunned actor's card also flinches. An optional Animator bool `Stunned` and trigger `StunFlinch` are supported; the procedural pose works without authored animation clips. Cleanse, expiry, defeat and reserve disable restore the model pose. Seal and other card restrictions do not masquerade as stun.

Accepted `StatusApplied` events produce a brief sword, shield or heart for ATK, DEF or maximum HP modifiers. Increases lift; decreases weaken/sink, with the DEF shield visibly splitting along a jagged crack. Health increase adds a plus. Color, silhouette and direction communicate the result. Stat visuals do not persist through the status clock, play on removal, or appear for rejected/ignored weaker applications. Bundles, signed potency, multipliers and serialized modifier mirrors resolve through Core's effective-stat calculation. Repeated grants reuse their actor's pooled effect. Combat state, HP recovery, damage and card legality remain Core-owned.

`StatusVisualData.ActorVisualEffect` is an optional presentation override; Automatic resolves gameplay semantics. Colors, size and timing are centralized in `Assets/Resources/ActorVisualEffectSettings.asset`. The Resources shader is included in builds. AVE geometry is excluded from camera/body bounds. Animation skip clears temporary effects; snapshot reconciliation recreates persistent effects. Scene cleanup releases generated meshes and materials.

Starting values: grant duration **0.95 s**, stun flinch **0.45 s**, icon size **0.9 world units**. Micro test: inspect all six icons from both planning cameras and apply a basic-stat bundle to an entire team. Pass when sword/shield/heart and increase/decrease direction can be distinguished during the hold, effects end within 1.2 s, and no actor or card feedback is obscured. If silhouettes are too small, raise IconSize; if grants overlap, lower IconSize or reduce spark spread. Adjust GrantDuration toward 1.1 s if the symbol is not readable before fading. The stun flinch should communicate a failed attempt while the actor stays in formation; lower flinch rotation if it resembles a hit reaction.

Use **Fighting Allstar → Preview Actor Visual Effects** to replay or scrub all six grants, stun and barrier in a disposable preview scene. **Fighting Allstar → Validation → Actor Visual Effects** runs semantic/lifecycle checks and writes `Logs/ActorVisualEffectEvidence/` captures. The grid has ATK/DEF/HP increases across the top and decreases below. `RunActorVisualEffectChecks.ps1` runs an isolated Unity Play Mode fixture covering timed expiry, turn-entry flinch, cleanup, real OnDisable, repeat grants, barrier synchronization and cinematic suppression, without opening the user's scene or run save.

Paralyze, Bleed, Poison, Shock and Ignite now use the same actor-owned lifecycle. Each accepted grant plays a short entrance followed by a persistent idle. Paralyze has yellow binding bands, electrical twitches and a restrained model pose; its disabled turn uses the shared flinch. Bleed sheds red teardrops. Poison has soft green mist and purple bubbles. Shock flashes cyan lightning along the body. Ignite emits orange flames with pale hot cores and rising embers. Billboards and a thin emitter band follow the visible side of the actor so particles remain readable from either camera. These effects use native Unity ParticleSystems and procedural shader silhouettes, without texture dependencies or colliders.

Idle activation is separate from grant feedback: loading/reconciling a snapshot starts idle only; accepted refreshes replay a bounded entrance on the existing root. Rejected and ignored weaker grants do not enter. Multiple stacks/sources share one visual per kind, retained until the last matching status is removed. Concurrent debuffs reduce emission by `1 / sqrt(active kinds)`. Cleanse, expiry, reserve, defeat and OnDisable clear the relevant systems and restore the model pose; animation skip clears entrances while preserving idle. Cinematics hide all persistent effects and restore idle without a new entrance. Generated particle materials and roots are released on actor destruction. Status recipes resolve by tags/IDs or the existing authored override; the historical `posion` ID remains supported. Ignite keeps its existing Core behavior (damage-received vulnerability); this implementation only visualizes it.

Starting values: entrance **0.7 s**, density **1**, idle particles capped at **28 per primary emitter / 18 per secondary emitter**, entrance cap **32**. Micro test: view each effect at normal planning distance from both teams' cameras, then give one actor all five and an entire team the same DOT. Pass when each silhouette/color is recognizable within one second, idle stays lighter than entrance, actor faces and status UI remain readable, and removing one source/last source gives the expected result. Lower DebuffParticleDensity if stacked statuses obscure the actor; increase it slightly if Bleed/Poison becomes difficult to recognize at battle distance. Paralyze should communicate an interrupted movement while preserving formation.

The preview includes individual debuffs and **All five debuffs**, with a 3.5 s scrub range. `debuff-enter-idle.png` shows Paralyze / Bleed / Poison / Shock / Ignite from left to right, entrances above and idle below; `all-debuffs-idle.png` captures simultaneous effects. Validation currently passes **92 editor checks** and **50 isolated Play Mode checks**, including actual particle emission beyond entrance expiry, accepted/rejected feedback, stacked-source removal, suppression, disable, actor destruction and reserve/death cleanup. No AVE runtime errors occurred. The batch fixture separately recorded one known Unity Editor SearchDatabase startup exception.
