# Actor-driven card execution

This contract implements the October 8 request and October 9 corrections, superseding earlier camera behavior in CameraReferences.md.

## October 9 fixed camera correction (current)

There are only three camera states: Rest, Wind Up and Attack. Each captures a fixed position, rotation and FOV. Card-phase changes cut directly to their captured shots; arena turn/rest transitions can still interpolate between saved endpoints, never track a moving actor or recipient. Attack is captured before approach using the planned contact position and a reserved reaction envelope from the recorded action, then stays unchanged through all hits, launch/fall/get-up and attacker return. Critical hits do not shake the camera. Ultimate energy plays within Wind Up without detail/hero camera states. Recovery has no separate camera shot or roll easing: self support also holds its Attack portrait rather than cutting behind. Queued cards enter their own Wind Up; an empty queue returns to the saved Rest view. The historical reference table and verification notes below describe earlier behavior where they conflict with this correction.

Starting reaction values and the floor-contact/multi-hit playtest are in [HitReactions.md](HitReactions.md). The fixed-shot tests reject changes in position, angle or FOV during displacement and recovery, on both sides and viewport orientations. This separates actor motion from camera motion so the force and height of a launch remain readable (game-design guidance).

Verified October 9 fixed-shot correction: the focused camera fixture passed 71,185 assertions, including both teams, portrait/landscape, self/team support, queued Wind Up cuts, fixed recovery and authored clip completion. All sixteen damage-template fixtures and the 3,693-check reaction fixture passed. Runtime compilation passed with zero warnings/errors. Per-frame assertion counts vary with Editor speed; no main scene or shared Animator asset was modified by these checks.

`ActorCardPresentation` owns Rest, WindUp, Action and Recovery. `BattleStagePresenter.Execution` creates one card context with the actor, category, rank, target-position snapshot and rear wind-up shot. The camera observes actor phase changes. Playback supplies category and target scope before preparation; damage, hit and status authority remain in Core.

Battle facing is derived from the positions of the two formation centers, so identical or stale anchor rotations cannot make both teams face the same way. `BattleFighterView` uses `actor / Model yaw correction / Rig + Animator`. The correction is above the Animator binding root, so an imported clip that resets rig yaw cannot erase it. The current sample art uses a 180-degree visual yaw correction (`CharacterObject.FighterModelYawOffset`); models authored facing +Z can set it to 0. Player and enemy models therefore face each other while camera shots and recovery use the stable actor direction. Formation refresh and reserve positioning use the same rule; changing the model's animated yaw cannot reverse the camera's rear/front reference. The camera fixture tests both model conventions and both sides, including the no-anchor spawn fallback.

| Card family | Rank 1 WindUp | Rank 2/3 WindUp | Action | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attack, AttackDebuff, Debuff, non-self Stance | Cut to captured rear shot; selected opponents turn toward actor | Cut to captured face/shoulder portrait | Cut to captured rear Attack shot; no tracking | Hold Attack shot through return |
| Self-target Stance | Cut to captured rear shot | Cut to actor portrait | Cut to captured rear Attack shot, then perform stance | Hold Attack shot |
| Self-target Buff, Recovery/heal | Skip animation preparation; cut immediately behind caster, with the full opposing formation ahead as for an AoE attack | Cut to actor portrait | Hold the exact preparation shot: rear at Rank 1, face at Rank 2/3 | Hold Attack shot for queue |
| Other Buff, Recovery/heal | Skip animation preparation; cut immediately to rear view of full allied formation | Cut immediately to the same allied team view during preparation | Hold the exact team shot, including single selected allies | Hold team shot through recovery and until next card/rest cut; no intermediate caster-rear reset |

Self targeting comes from `EffectTargetScope.Self` in production; standalone preview calls infer one recipient matching the source. A single selected ally remains a team-support shot. The target focus is a captured world position/bounds and does not track recoil, rotation, death or later combo target changes. AoE captures the whole opposing formation even with one survivor; damage still resolves against living recipients. Reserves enter the formation registry after landing, while defeated slots retain their geometry.

Attack composition uses a rear shoulder camera and a target-position look point. It frames the target row fully while allowing the caster's lower body to extend into the foreground/HUD area. This preserves the reference relationship instead of fitting two full bodies into a high, distant view. Rank 3 action roll stays fixed through recovery. Every queue-empty return uses the overhead drafting view, deliberately different from the clips' rear rest view.

## October 8 reference observations

Inspected local `Rank-Card-ex.mp4`, `stance-card-ex.mp4`, `BuffHeal-Card-ex.mp4` and `ult_session.mp4` frame-by-frame, including native portrait frames and dense contact sheets in `Temp/CameraReferences/October8`.

- Rank-card 3–4 s and 9–12 s: caster in the lower left foreground; victim ahead near the center, shallow downward pitch; coral total in the upper right. Rank-card 8 s: front face/body anticipation before returning to rear action.
- Stance 4–6 s: front anticipation/cast detail; 7 s: rear settled caster. The user's self-target exception takes precedence: Action moves to that caster's rear instead of cutting to a whole-team support shot.
- Buff/heal 18–20 s and 28–30 s: ranked portrait and cast reveal. The October 9 correction takes precedence: non-self support holds the allied team shot at all ranks; self support holds its ranked face shot through Action.
- Ultimate 0–0.7 s: compact red ENEMY TURN above the hand while the arena camera changes sides. The implementation synchronizes the announcement with a polar orbit to the incoming side's overhead drafting position.
- Ultimate 9–9.75 s: black/magenta diagonal portrait band and speed streaks; 10.5 s: ground/body detail; 11.25–12 s: rising energy and low hero reveal; 12.75–15 s: attack/impact; by 15.75 s: battle framing. The implementation uses existing fighter artwork and an original reusable energy reveal. It does not reproduce the reference character's bespoke mount animation.

Turn announcements now use cyan/player or coral/enemy text, an underline, a short impact entrance and directional exit above the tray. Other opening notices retain a separate panel style. TOTAL DAMAGE is a UIToolkit widget with a coral impact burst, outlined number, hit pulse and release fade. It accumulates calculated damage from every hit of one card/counter, including overkill after a recipient reaches zero HP, stays visible through long multi-hit actions, and resets even when the next card has the same owner. Large totals preserve every digit and saturate at `long.MaxValue`.

The ultimate sequence first recovers any previous actor, clears camera tracking and snap-cuts to the ultimate owner's side of the overhead drafting/rest view, even when more cards are queued. It hides hand/phase controls and plays the charge cue during a short arena-wide anticipation pause, then enters the existing portrait cut-in, actor WindUp and world reveal before rear Action. The arena remains at rest throughout the portrait overlay; there is no extra return to rest between portrait and actor reveal. Damage/target feedback remains visible during impact. Recovery restores the HUD. Skip/disable clears the anticipation flag, overlay and world energy and restores pose/camera/UI. This is presentation-only and consumes no extra gameplay resources.

Starting value for the overhead ultimate pause: 350 ms. Playtest an attack → ultimate → buff queue and two consecutive ultimates on both sides. Pass if an observer notices the full-arena pause before the portrait and can identify a larger action approaching; the pause must remain skippable. Increase the pause in 50 ms steps if it is missed; shorten it if consecutive ultimates make the queue feel slow. Readability comes from the reset to full-arena framing, satisfaction from the charge sound followed by the existing reveal, and response from shared skip/disable cleanup.

## Transitions and cleanup

- Rest enters WindUp on CardPlayed/CounterStarted for offensive cards and ranked support. Rank 1 support enters Action directly.
- WindUp exits after preparation feedback and an authored clip's completion, when present. Action begins the appropriate Attack/Debuff/Stance/Buff/Heal clip or procedural cast/strike.
- Action continues through all hit and after-damage barriers. AfterAction recovers the actor before after-action effects. ActionCompleted waits for remaining feedback; an empty current-turn card/counter queue returns to the drafting top view. Turn/status/battle boundaries terminate that queue even when later turns contain cards. A pending execution holds the current recovered shot (team view for non-self support) until the next actor's preparation.
- Recovery returns position, rotation and scale to formation. It waits for an authored Recovery clip when present, then enters Rest. All camera positions, angles and FOV remain at the captured Attack shot until the next Wind Up or queue-empty Rest.
- Skip, restore and disable stop motion, reset actor phase/pose, unsubscribe the camera observer and restore Rest. Missing actors/animators and absent state clips use procedural feedback. Animation/motion watchdogs prevent broken clips from indefinitely blocking playback.
- Presentation phases consume no gameplay resources. Card costs and interruption/outcome decisions remain authoritative combat events.

## Animation integration

Optional Animator states or triggers: `WindUp`, `Action`, `Recovery`, `Idle`, plus category triggers `Attack`, `Debuff`, `Stance`, `Buff`, `Heal`. The component uses the category state/trigger first, then `Action`. Legacy sample controllers with no parameters reuse their `_skill1` and `_idle` states. Unauthored WindUp uses a visible anticipation lean/squash plus the existing charge cue; Action uses existing strike/cast feedback; Recovery supplies a settling motion. No new animation art is required for the phases to function. Existing `CardFirstHit`/`CardLastHit` markers continue synchronizing authored attacks; generated multi-hit templates retain their own timing.

Starting values: offensive Rank 1 preparation 180 ms; ranked preparation retains 650/850 ms; recovery retains 320 ms with a 2.5% fallback settling pulse. Wind Up/Attack entry cuts directly to the snapshot. Rear shoulder starts 0.35 model heights to the side, 1.3 heights above ground and 1.8 heights behind, then adjusts depth once to fit the captured target/reaction envelope. Close attacks open the lateral angle toward 1.7 heights; a size-based minimum offset keeps a clear ray to the victim when wide AoE framing increases rear distance. Support queue/rest exits remain instantaneous cuts. Other turn orbits take 750 ms with the announcement overlapping them. Watchdogs retain 8 seconds. These are placeholder tuning values, not measured 7DS constants. Test ten alternating cards: pass if anticipation is visible, no target is obscured during impact, and each recovery clearly ends before the next preparation. Shorten preparation if the queue drags; increase it if anticipation is missed. Increase lateral clearance if a victim is occluded; reduce the settling pulse if it looks elastic.
