# Combat wording and icon contract

Player goal: predict an action's damage basis, targets, subsequent effects and timing from any card or character view.

- `CombatTextCatalog` owns display spelling, explicit aliases, stat names and keyword explanations. Its evidence is the attack/status rows in `Doc_7DSGCCopyCat/Docs(Template)/3_Outputs/Specs/CharacterData/source-catalog.json`, the combat source contract and the executable attack rules. For example, Quell scales with Stances; Cleave scales with Crit Damage above 100% and cannot crit. Recovery Rate and Pierce Rate are stats, not Recovery/Pierce effects.
- `CardEffectText` describes supported executable operations in action timing order: before-action effects, damage, then follow-up effects. Damage uses `Inflict [keyword] damage equal to XX% of the hero's [stat] on [target]`. Targets use `one enemy`, `one ally`, `all enemies`, `all allies` or `the hero`. Healing/support cards describe their actual action. Chances, stack counts, durations, scaling and stat modifiers come from the effect payload.
- `CardDescriptionText` preserves complex authored rules, such as conditional additional damage, stat theft, shared chances and Stance reactions. It normalizes their damage clause while retaining the supplementary prose. Before-action effects remain explicitly before damage. This adapter is intentional: some card rules live in character reactions rather than the card sequence, so rebuilding only from the sequence would omit them.
- `PassiveDescriptionText` generates simple aura sentences from executable modifiers and target filters. Reaction passives retain edited authored prose. Aura/stat contributions do not create passive status chips. Actual applied named statuses can still have a badge and explanation.
- Catalog `Description` fields contain canonical display text; card `SourceDescription` fields and the original source catalog retain source wording. Battle tooltips, the character inspector, character details, ultimate previews and dungeon enemy details use the same formatting pipeline. Formatting is idempotent and does not mutate executable effects.
- Icon resolution uses exact IDs or explicit/normalized aliases within the requested polarity. Stat modifiers map to authored artwork IDs independently of display wording, including HP, stat groups, damage reduction and multiplier decreases. It never guesses a status from a substring in prose. Sprite caches include polarity. Existing named status art remains in use; generic up/down/Neutral stat assets provide a polarity-safe fallback where no dedicated asset exists. The catalog builder preserves authored Debuff polarity for increases to damage taken.
- Damage animation settings still select the authored close/long, single-target/area and hit-count template. Hit count is excluded from card information only; it is not removed from animation or combat resolution.

Clarity is the relevant game-design check: the action, its conditions and its icons agree. Response, motivation, satisfaction and game identity are unchanged. No combat amounts, targeting, clocks or animation settings are edited. The builder's polarity fix restores the classification already authored on the recipe and therefore also preserves its intended cleanse/immunity treatment.

Run `Tests/CombatPresentation/RunCombatTextChecks.ps1`. It launches a separate hidden Unity fixture, checks all runtime-ready characters and card ranks/tiers, and writes `Artifacts/CombatText/Review.md`. Coverage includes aliases, compound-token matching, repeat formatting, executable-data preservation, pre-action timing, exact stat/named sprite identity, multiplier direction, potency scaling, Neutral healing statuses and Kensou's Recovery Rate passive. The original open project and player saves are not used by the fixture.

Readability check: inspect Kensou's passive, a plain attack, a ranked damage/debuff card, a healing card, Cassandra's pre-damage Ultimate and a conditional/shared-chance card. The passive should retain its portrait/restriction indicators without an aura chip; damage bases and targets should read consistently; applied status icons should match the effect. Long special descriptions remain scrollable in their existing panels.

Verified 2026-10-15 after restoring animation templates and stat sprite mappings: the isolated Unity runtime compiled and passed 3,477 checks across 34 characters and 408 card ranks/tiers. All sixteen animation templates also passed configured-hit playback, framing, recovery and skip checks. These fixtures do not replace checking final character animation feel in the live scene.

## Existing source/runtime differences exposed by generated text

The current authored runtime values differ from source prose in places: Athena R1/R2 Recovery Rate reductions are 0.4%/0.6%; Kyo94's aura is 5% per enemy Ignite, capped at 50%; Kyo98's reduction is 5% per enemy Ignite, capped at 50%; Heavy D's aura scales from 100% of his Crit Resistance. Generated text reports executable values. This refactor does not rebalance those values to the source catalog.

The broader combat runner currently fails the synthetic multi-hit assertion `First-hit status ran late` in `MultiHitChecks.cs`. The same assertion fails when built from the unchanged HEAD core/tests in an isolated baseline. Keep its combat-timing repair separate from this presentation change.
