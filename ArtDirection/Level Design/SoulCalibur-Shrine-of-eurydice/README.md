## 1. Visual Concept & Narrative Setting
A synthesis of two iconic Soulcalibur concepts:
1. **The Floating Lake Arena:** An ancient carved stone plinth/raft floating submerged on the surface of a crystalline alpine lake (surrounded by rushing water currents and carved elephant foundation reliefs).
2. **Shrine of Eurydice (Cloud Sanctuary):** A monumental classical Hellenic mountaintop sanctuary perched on the precipice of a bottomless cloud abyss, framed by cascading waterfalls, marble colonnades, a winged Goddess statue raising a golden blade, and colossal Spartan sentinels holding blue flame braziers.

### Key Visual Pillars
* **Neoclassical Splendor:** Translucent white statuary marble, gold leaf / brass inlays, and Corinthian columns entwined with mountain ivy.
* **Cascading Hydrodynamics:** Multi-tiered marble steps with continuous water flows feeding into the lake reservoir, kicking up soft mist with prismatic rainbow refractions.
* **Colossal Scale & Abyss:** Towering mountain peaks rising above a thick sea of rolling clouds and deep foggy gorges guarded by monumental warrior statues.

## 3. Level Design & Camera Calibration
* **Combat Floor:** Symmetrical floating platform (~16m × 16m) with low-relief geometric marble paving; water laps gently at the infinity edge where it plunges into the foggy abyss.
* **Planning Camera:** Positioned behind the player party looking across the platform toward the Eurydice waterfall stairs and snow-capped mountain.
* **Attack Camera:** Sweeping lateral camera movements that emphasize the dizzying vertical drop into the cloud sea and the monumental Spartan statues.
