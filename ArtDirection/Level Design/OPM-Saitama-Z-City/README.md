## 1. Visual Concept & Narrative Setting
Set in the deserted "Ghost Town" area of City Z from *One-Punch Man*, where Saitama lives. The combat area is an abandoned residential roadway running underneath a towering concrete highway viaduct. It captures a distinct juxtaposition of mundane suburban Japanese infrastructure with the destructive aftermath of monster encounters.

### Key Visual Pillars
* **Cel-Shading & Inking:** 2-step cel ramp shader with clean black ink outlines and flat, uncluttered albedo textures.
* **Quiet Abandonment:** Broad empty asphalt street, weathered concrete barriers, utility poles, dangling power lines, and an automated coin-parking booth.
* **Monumental Framing:** The left sky is framed by the heavy concrete pillars and box girders of the elevated highway, while the right is framed by Saitama's 5-story apartment building.

## 3. Level Design & Camera Calibration
* **Combat Floor:** Clear asphalt roadway (~18m × 8m) with painted crosswalk lines. Absolutely zero vertical obstruction in the combat lane to keep character foot silhouettes and targeting reticles 100% visible.
* **Planning Camera:** Positioned behind the player team looking slightly down-street (-28° pitch, ~12m distance, ~8m height).
* **Attack Camera:** Dynamic lateral cuts tracking attacker lunges against the rhythm of the apartment balconies and highway pillars.
