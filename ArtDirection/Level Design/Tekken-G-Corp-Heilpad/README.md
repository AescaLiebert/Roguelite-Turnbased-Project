## 1. Visual Concept & Narrative Setting
Set on the high-altitude rooftop helipad of G Corporation's headquarters skyscraper at midnight during a violent storm. It combines heavy industrial brutalism with cyberpunk mega-corporate aesthetics. A black military attack gunship hovers immediately off the platform, cutting through the torrential downpour with its twin searchlights.

### Key Visual Pillars
* **Midnight Rain & Specular Wetness:** Puddle-masked roughness maps, planar/screen-space reflections, dynamic water droplets, and rain splashes on metal grating.
* **Hard-Surface Steel Industrialism:** Anti-slip diamond plate flooring, hexagonal drainage grates, steel pipe guardrails, and heavy rivet plates.
* **High-Contrast Warning Palette:** Deep gunmetal and charcoal navy tones punctuated by stark crimson red hazard beacons and cold cyan LED runway guide strips.

## 3. Level Design & Camera Calibration
* **Combat Floor:** Multi-tiered industrial deck (~16m × 10m). The main combat floor is a flat steel plate deck bordered by hexagonal grates with low stairs leading to a raised circular landing zone in the rear.
* **Planning Camera:** Centered behind the player party looking toward the edge of the rooftop, framing the hovering gunship and illuminated skyscraper towers.
* **Attack Camera:** Low-angle hero cuts tracking combat strikes into the searchlight beams, catching rain streaks and red beacon halos.
