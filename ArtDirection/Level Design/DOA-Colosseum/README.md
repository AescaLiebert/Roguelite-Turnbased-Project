## 1. Visual Concept & Narrative Setting
A premier high-tech global martial arts amphitheater inspired by the *Dead or Alive 6* "DOA Colosseum". The stage fuses ancient Roman Colosseum limestone arches and monumental winged guardian sculptures with high-tech sports entertainment infrastructure: electronic LED ticker screens, suspended stadium floodlights, roaring spectator crowds, and electrified boundary ropes.

### Key Visual Pillars
* **Modern Classical Fusion:** Polished Roman limestone porticos and colossal white marble winged statues integrated with modern steel roof trusses and corporate skyscrapers.
* **Electrified "Danger Zone" Boundary:** Technical stanchions connected by glowing pink/red electrified neon ropes framing the circular fighting ring.
* **High-Spectacle Championship Energy:** Sunlit midday atmosphere, drifting celebration confetti, lens flares, camera flashes from the grandstands, and bright red center combat canvas.

## 3. Level Design & Camera Calibration
* **Combat Floor:** Elevated circular ring (~14m diameter) with a vibrant, high-gloss red composite mat and crisp white concentric circles. Absolutely clear of surface debris to provide optimal clarity for turn-based tactical indicators.
* **Planning Camera:** Positioned behind the allied party looking across the ring toward the VIP stands and the arched "DOA COLOSSEUM" superstructure.
* **Attack Camera:** Dynamic upward-tilted cameras framing attacks against the colossal winged statues and the roaring stadium crowd.
